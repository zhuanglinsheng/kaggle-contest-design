# Review and method records

This directory retains dated review comments, intermediate research logs and
executable method/source records. Read a statement against its manuscript
version, date and named calculation. Earlier Pending, unclear or unverified
labels describe that review stage; use the current [reproduction guide](../REPRODUCIBILITY.md)
and [figure/table index](../PAPER_EVIDENCE.csv) for the maintained workflow.

Original reviews are retained as reviews, not rewritten into current findings.
Frozen Python/Stan/C++ snapshots and executable Markdown methods preserve their
recorded content hashes. Their comments and status strings also retain that
historical context. Current producers and long-term result descriptions state
their concrete inputs, outputs and diagnostic roles.

Specific numerical outcomes remain explicit: failed candidate solves, comparison
intervals, draw counts and unresolved design cells keep their recorded values.
A finite design search is described by its evaluated domain. These statuses are
independent of whether generated chains or caches are distributed through Git.
